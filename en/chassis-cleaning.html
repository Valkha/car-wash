Underbody

Chassis &
engine cleaning

What you never see and the inspector looks at first. Chassis, engine bay and wheel arches jet-cleaned, at a fixed price.

Fixed price 159.90 CHF incl. VAT

October offer

20% off this service until 31 October 2026, for the first 100 customers. That is CHF 127.90 incl. VAT instead of the usual rate. The discount comes off the balance settled on site.

01The service

The three areas covered

Chassis cleaning

Underbody cleared of mud, salt and built-up deposits.

Engine cleaning

Engine bay degreased, with the usual protection over sensitive components.

Between the wheels

Wheel arches and housings, where corrosion always starts.

02Location

Where the work is done

At our workshop

In our Geneva workshop, on a lift. The lift is essential: it is the only way to reach the whole underbody, which is why this service is not carried out at your place.

Workshop only.

Book this service

You book your slot online and pay a deposit of CHF 47.95, which is 30% of the price. The balance of CHF 111.95 is settled on site once the work is done.

The slot you book matches the actual duration of the work, not just a drop-off appointment.

Allow around 2 hours. The vehicle is handed straight back. Ideal before the inspection: a clean underbody is quicker to check and hides no defect.
