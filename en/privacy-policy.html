Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Collection of Personal Data

As part of the use of our website and to carry out our services, we collect data related to the pick-up and delivery of the vehicle to the customer's home or business. We also collect the necessary information to send quotes and for subscribing to our newsletter.

2. Purpose of Collection

The data collected is strictly reserved for the internal use of the independent company AHMED-YAHIA LM, managed by Ichem Ahmed-Yahia, operating under the name CLEAN CARS WASH Geneva. This data is used to:

  • Respond to requests and send quotes.
  • Organize the pick-up and delivery of the vehicle in the Geneva and Vaud areas (travel included in Geneva, flat fee by zone in Vaud).
  • Send our newsletter including discounts (e.g., -10%, -15%, -30% for birthdays).

We guarantee that there is no sale of your data to businesses for commercial canvassing.

3. Information Security

Data entered in our forms is transmitted via the secure Web3Forms API to reach us directly by email. We apply the necessary security standards to protect your information against unauthorized access during transmission.

The travel-fee calculator on the home page sends the address you type to the Swiss Confederation's public geodata service (geo.admin.ch, swisstopo), only to suggest addresses and find the district. Clean Cars Wash does not store this address: the result is kept in your browser for the duration of your visit.

4. Giveaway

For the giveaway running from 1 to 30 November 2026, we only use the data already provided when booking (name and contact details) of customers who received a service during that period, in order to draw up the list of entrants, remove duplicates, run the prize draw and contact the winner. This data is not used for any other purpose. You may decline to take part at any time before the draw by writing to info@clean-cars-wash.ch. See the giveaway rules.

5. Data Retention Period

In accordance with the GDPR, we retain your data for the time strictly necessary to process your commercial file, with a maximum limit of three (3) years after our last exchange.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us by email:

info@clean-cars-wash.ch
