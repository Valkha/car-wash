How to enter

  • Book, pay for and have a Clean Cars Wash service carried out between 1 and 30 November 2026, at the usual price.
  • Only services carried out and paid in full during that period count.
  • One entry per person, whatever the number of services or the amount spent.
  • Open to adults resident in Switzerland, and to cross-border residents of France who receive a service in Switzerland.

Official rules

  1. Organiser. The giveaway is organised by AHMED-YAHIA LM, operating Clean Cars Wash, Chemin des Vergers, 1208 Geneva.
  2. Duration. From 1 November 2026 at 00:00 to 30 November 2026 at 23:59.
  3. Who may enter. Adults (18 or over) resident in Switzerland, and cross-border residents of France who receive a Clean Cars Wash service in Switzerland.
  4. How to enter. Entry is exclusively by booking, paying for and having a Clean Cars Wash service carried out during the giveaway period. Only services actually carried out and paid in full between 1 and 30 November 2026 count: a service booked in November but carried out in December does not qualify. Services are charged at the usual price; no surcharge is required to enter.
  5. One entry per person. Each person has a single chance to win, whatever the number of services or the amount spent. Duplicates are removed.
  6. Opting out. Any customer may decline to take part, without giving a reason, by writing to info@clean-cars-wash.ch before the draw.
  7. Prize. One new iPhone 18 Pro Max, 256 GB, purple. There is a single winner.
  8. Draw. On 22 December 2026, at random, among eligible entrants. Proof of the draw is kept.
  9. Winner. The winner is contacted personally by phone or e-mail and has seven days to reply. Their identity is not made public without their consent.
  10. Personal data. The giveaway only uses data already provided when booking (name and contact details), to draw up the list of entrants, run the draw and contact the winner. It is not used for any other purpose. See the privacy policy.
  11. Contact. Any question: info@clean-cars-wash.ch or +41 78 265 70 71.

How the winner is chosen

Prize draw on 22 December 2026 among eligible entrants. The winner is contacted personally.
