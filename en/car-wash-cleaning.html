FAQ

Car wash and cleaning: your questions

How long does a wash take?+
Allow 1 h 30 for the Silver Pack, 2 h 30 for the Gold Pack and 3 h 30 for the Diamond Pack.
Can surcharges apply?+
Yes, in two cases, listed above: an extremely dirty vehicle, and pet hair removal.
How long should I wait after a seat shampoo?+
Allow at least 4 to 6 hours of drying before intensive use. We recommend leaving the windows slightly open to help evaporation.
Where is the wash carried out?+
In our workshop at Chemin des Vergers, in Geneva, or at your home or office, on a private space with a 230V outlet. Travel is included in Geneva; in Vaud, a flat fee of CHF 39.90 or 59.90 applies depending on the district.
How does payment work?+
You pay a 30% deposit online, by TWINT or card, via SumUp, then the balance on site. Up to 24 hours before the appointment, the deposit is carried over to a future booking if you cancel; less than 24 hours before, it is kept.
