Practical questions

Payment & how it works

What payment methods do you accept?+
A 30% deposit is required online at the time of booking via our secure SumUp link (credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay). The balance is settled at the end of the service.
Can I cancel?+
Yes. Up to 24 hours before the appointment, cancelling costs nothing: the deposit is carried over to a future booking. Less than 24 hours before, it is kept.
Are there any additional fees?+
An additional charge of CHF 74.60 incl. VAT per hour applies for extremely dirty vehicles. We also offer pet hair removal (from CHF 49.90 incl. VAT).
How long should I wait after a seat shampoo?+
Allow a minimum of 4 to 6 hours of drying time before heavy use. In summer, drying is usually faster. We recommend leaving the windows slightly open to help ventilation.
