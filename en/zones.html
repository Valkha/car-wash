Service areas
& travel fees

We operate across the entire canton of Geneva and the canton of Vaud. Travel is entirely included in Geneva. In Vaud, a flat fee applies depending on the district — quoted in advance, with no surprises.

Travel included

From Chancy to Hermance, Céligny included: no travel fee.

03How it works

What to remember

Calculated per district

The fee depends on your commune's district, not on the exact distance. You know the amount before you book.

Once per trip

The fee is added once per trip, whichever pack you choose.

Settled on site

The online deposit stays at 30% of the service price. The travel fee is settled on site, together with the balance.

Amounts include 8.1% Swiss VAT. Can't see your commune clearly on the map? Call us and we'll confirm your zone straight away.
