Practical questions

The at-home service in practice

How does home pick-up work?+
We pick up your vehicle from your home or office (across Geneva and Vaud) and return it after the service. Travel is included within the canton of Geneva; in Vaud, the zone fee applies — see the zone map.
What do I need to prepare for a home service?+
A private parking space and a 230V power outlet (required), a water point (recommended). If no parking or outlet is available on our arrival, the full service fee remains due and will be invoiced.
Do you operate in bad weather?+
Yes, we operate in all weather conditions thanks to our professional equipment. In case of extreme conditions (storm, snow), we contact you to adapt the protocol or agree on a free reschedule.
