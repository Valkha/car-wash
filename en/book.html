At home

What you need to prepare

  • 🅿️

    Private parking space Required

    Garage, driveway, or residential parking. Car washing on public roads is illegal in Switzerland.

  • ⚡

    230V power outlet Required

    Accessible from the vehicle's location for the full duration of the service.

  • 💧

    Water point Recommended

    Not essential depending on the chosen service — our team will confirm at booking.

By confirming your booking, you confirm these conditions are met at the given address. If our team cannot intervene on arrival (no parking, no outlet, or no vehicle access), the full service fee remains due and will be invoiced.
