Bodywork protection

PPF
paint protection film

Protect your bodywork so the paint stays perfect. A clear film applied over the lacquer, taking the damage in its place.

From 999.00 CHF incl. VAT

Your vehicle stays off the road

Allow one full day minimum, more for a fully covered vehicle. The car stays in the workshop while the film is applied: plan alternative transport.

20% off this service until 31 October 2026, for the first 100 customers. That is CHF 799.20 incl. VAT instead of the usual rate. The discount comes off the balance settled on site.

01The service

What the film gives you

Protection against scratches and impacts

The film absorbs what would have marked the lacquer: rubbing, door scuffs, small knocks.

Resistance to stone chips

Front end, sills, bonnet edges — the areas that take everything on the motorway.

Preserves the original paint

The factory lacquer stays untouched underneath, which matters at resale.

Clear, discreet finish

Once fitted, the film is invisible. The original colour and gloss are kept.

02Location

Where the work is done

At our workshop

Film application requires a dust-free environment: this service is carried out in our workshop only.

Workshop only.

Book this service

You book your appointment online and pay a deposit of CHF 299.70, which is 30% of the base rate. The balance is confirmed after an inspection and settled on site.

The 30-minute slot you book is a drop-off appointment — time to hand the vehicle over and go through the details. The work itself takes one full day minimum.

The quote depends on which panels are covered: front end only, partial kit or full vehicle. The price shown corresponds to front-end protection.
