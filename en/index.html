Technical questions

Options, polish, ceramic, PPF

What is the difference between polishing and a ceramic coating?+
Polishing corrects the paint (swirl marks, wash marks); ceramic protects it and makes water bead off. Ceramic always comes after the correction: that is what the Ceramic Polish Pack combines, from CHF 999.00 incl. VAT on quote. It is all explained in our article.
How long is the vehicle immobilised for a ceramic polish?+
Allow one full day minimum, often more depending on the paint. The ceramic polish is carried out in the workshop, or at your place in a closed garage: ceramic tolerates neither dust nor rain while it cures.
What is PPF?+
A clear film that takes the stone chips and scratches instead of your paint. Application requires a dust-free environment: it is carried out in the workshop only, and takes one full day minimum. PPF in detail.
Does wrapping touch the paint?+
No: wrapping changes the colour of the vehicle without touching the paint — matt, gloss, satin or carbon. It is carried out in the workshop; allow one full day minimum. Wrapping in detail.
What interior options are there?+
The Gold Pack adds seat and carpet shampoo and plastic renovation; the Diamond Pack, leather or Alcantara care and an anti-odour treatment. Pet hair removal is optional. After a shampoo, allow 4 to 6 hours of drying before heavy use.
What is a starlight headliner?+
Optical fibres laid by hand in the headliner, four colours, brightness adjustable by remote. The headliner has to be removed: the service is carried out in the workshop and takes half a day minimum. The starlight headliner in detail.
How do I keep the result after a detailing?+
With regular washing: combos include a Silver maintenance wash within 30 days, and subscriptions cost −10% to −27% less than one-off washes.
