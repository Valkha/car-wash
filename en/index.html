    Frequently asked questions

    Another question? Call us on 078 265 70 71.

    What do I need for a wash at home?+

    A private parking space (garage, driveway or residential car park) and a 230V power socket, both required; a water point is recommended. At the workshop, you don’t need anything.

    How much is the travel fee?+

    Nothing at the workshop. At home, travel is included across the canton of Geneva. In Vaud, a flat fee is added depending on the district: CHF 39.90 (Nyon, Morges, Ouest lausannois, Lausanne, Gros-de-Vaud) or CHF 59.90 for the other districts. It is shown before you pay.

    How does payment work?+

    You choose your pack, the place and your time slot, then pay a 30% deposit online by TWINT or card, secured by SumUp. The balance is settled on site. Up to 24 hours before the appointment, the deposit is carried over to a future booking. Less than 24 hours before, it is retained.

    How do I enter the giveaway?+

    A service carried out and paid for between 1 and 30 November 2026 is all it takes: you are entered automatically, one entry per person. Draw on 22 December 2026.

    What is the difference between a polish and a ceramic coating?+

    A polish corrects the paint (swirls, wash marks); a ceramic coating protects it and makes water bead. The coating always comes after the correction: that is what the Polish Ceramic Pack combines, from CHF 999 incl. VAT on quotation.

    What is PPF?+

    A clear film that takes stone chips and scratches instead of your paint. Fitting it requires a dust-free environment: it is done exclusively at the workshop and takes at least one full day.
